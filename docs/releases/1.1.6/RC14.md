Status: **FROZEN; SUPERSEDED BY RC15; NOT PUBLISHED**.

Bounded RC14 acceptance verified the native stale-save correction and found an
additional malformed-input boundary: an authenticated `content[]` request was
left to Core, which erased the post on the minimum PHP/Core combination. The
same result occurred with the plugin disabled. [RC15](RC15.md) adds a narrow
fail-closed check for saved clocks rather than importing RC14 acceptance.
RC14 results remain in `.local/rc14-independent-20261113/` and related workstream
directories. Its 12 native stale/fresh pairs, Plugin Check and browser results
are historical results for this exact archive, not a full-release GO.

RC14 addresses the demonstrated
[older Classic document save loss](../../defects/CLASSIC-STALE-SAVE-P1-02.md).
It prevents ambiguous removal of previously saved clocks by an old native
Classic post form or its autosave, while preserving intentional deletion from
a fresh form. Rejected manual saves expose escaped submitted text for recovery;
they do not silently merge or replace user edits.
